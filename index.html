Doctoral Researcher · TU Munich

Sebastian Bitzer

I work on code-based post-quantum cryptography. My work has contributed to CROSS, which reached the second round of NIST's additional signature process, and to recent advances in HQC decoding and DFR analysis.

Email Google Scholar DBLP GitHub CV (PDF)

Research

HQC beyond the standard

Reducing public-key and ciphertext sizes of HQC through generalized concatenated codes and reliability-based errors-and-erasures decoding, with refined decryption-failure-rate analysis.

Restricted decoding & CROSS

Zero-knowledge protocols and signatures from the restricted syndrome decoding problem, underlying the CROSS submission to NIST, with follow-up work on TCitH/VOLEitH-based variants.

Metrics, bounds & decoding

Bounds and codes in the weighted-Hamming, Lee, and cover metrics; information-set decoding with hints and template side-channel attacks on code-based signature schemes.
